Rook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Rook ("Rook", "the bot", or "the application") may process or store when you interact with it on Discord.

Rook is an independently developed Discord application. By using Rook, you acknowledge that some information is required for the bot to provide its features.

1. Information Rook May Process

Depending on the features used in a Discord server, Rook may process or store information including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord message IDs
  • Bot configuration and server settings
  • Streak and transaction-related records
  • Lottery entries, ticket counts, payments and verification records
  • Rook economy and wallet records
  • Room and game configuration
  • Feedback and bug reports submitted through Rook
  • Administrative and moderation records required for Rook features

Rook does not require your real name, physical address, telephone number, payment card information, or other similar real-world identification information in order to use its normal features.

2. Message Content

Some Rook features rely on processing Discord messages in order to detect supported commands, transactions, game events, streak activity, or responses from supported bots.

Rook may temporarily process message content to determine whether a message matches an event or transaction that the bot needs to track.

When necessary for features such as transaction or result verification, Rook may store information associated with the relevant matched message, such as its message ID, channel ID, author or bot ID, timestamp, and a limited snapshot of relevant content.

Rook is not intended to archive ordinary conversations or create a general history of server messages.

3. Why This Information Is Used

Information processed by Rook is used to operate features such as:

  • Tracking supported game activity and streaks
  • Recording transactions and results
  • Operating lotteries and verifying entries
  • Maintaining Rook economy balances and related records
  • Maintaining server-specific configuration
  • Operating rooms and other Rook game features
  • Preventing duplicate or incorrect processing
  • Investigating bugs and technical problems
  • Preventing abuse or misuse of Rook
  • Improving the reliability of the application

4. Lotteries and Transactions

When participating in a Rook-managed lottery or related feature, Rook may record information required to operate and verify the activity. This may include your Discord user ID, ticket quantity, transaction information, verification status, related Discord message IDs, and timestamps.

These records are used to determine valid participation, prevent duplicate entries, perform draws, and maintain the integrity of the feature.

5. Rook Economy

If you use Rook's virtual economy features, Rook may store records such as your Discord user ID, virtual balance, transaction history, rewards, or other information necessary to maintain the economy.

Rook's virtual currency and game records have no guaranteed real-world monetary value.

6. Feedback and Bug Reports

If you voluntarily submit feedback or a bug report through Rook, the bot may store the information you provide together with relevant Discord identifiers and technical context needed to investigate the report.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in bug reports, feedback, or other submissions to Rook.

7. Data Retention

Rook retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its features, maintain records required for those features, investigate problems, or protect the integrity of the application.

Certain tracking records may be subject to automated retention periods or cleanup processes. Other records, such as active configuration, economy balances, or records necessary to maintain ongoing features, may remain stored while they are still required.

Data may also be deleted when it is no longer required, when a feature is discontinued, or following an appropriate deletion request.

8. Data Sharing

Rook does not sell personal data.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers used to operate Rook, such as database or hosting providers, where necessary for the bot to function.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the security and integrity of Rook.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by Rook. However, no online service, database, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Access and Deletion Requests

If you have a question about information associated with your Discord account or would like to request deletion of applicable Rook data, you may contact the Rook developer directly through Discord.

You may also use the Report Bug option available through /rook help to establish contact regarding a data or privacy concern.

Please state clearly that your request concerns privacy or data deletion. Additional information may be requested where reasonably necessary to verify the Discord account associated with the request.

Some information may need to be retained where it is necessary for security, abuse prevention, maintaining the integrity of shared records, or complying with applicable obligations.

11. Discord

Rook operates through Discord and therefore also relies on information made available through Discord's platform and APIs.

Your use of Discord itself is governed separately by Discord's own terms, policies, and privacy practices.

12. Children's Privacy

Rook is intended to be used only by individuals who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable terms and age requirements.

Rook does not knowingly seek to collect real-world personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Rook's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when a material revision is published.

14. Contact

Questions, privacy concerns, and applicable data requests may be directed to the Rook developer through Discord.

Users may also initiate contact using the Report Bug option in /rook help.

Rook is an independently developed Discord application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.
